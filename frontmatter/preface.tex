This project was born from a desire to have an accessible, compact Greek Bible available in
print. While many fine Greek New Testaments exist in printed form, the Septuagint has been far
less accessible---particularly in a format that is both portable and minimalist. Most printed
Septuagints are large and expensive. And a complete Greek Bible, containing both the Old and New
Testaments under one cover, has been especially difficult to find at a reasonable price. I
undertook this project for anyone who, like me, wants a physical Greek Bible as a companion for
study and devotion.

In designing this edition, I was inspired by the minimalist reader Bibles available in English.
My aim was to keep the text front and center, eliminating distractions and unnecessary elements.
Section headings and chapter numbers have been made as unobtrusive as possible. I ultimately
decided to retain verse numbers for ease of navigation, but they are visually subdued. The goal
is a novel-like reading experience, where the text of Scripture speaks for itself.

I am grateful to my wife Amanda for her encouragement and patience throughout this project. I 
also wish to thank Cody Weber, Juan Arambula, and Andrew DeWitt for reviewing corrections to 
the Septuagint text. The open-source community has been indispensable: the Brenton LXX \LaTeX\ 
Print Project by mrgreekgeek provided the original digitized Septuagint text, and Eliran Wong's 
OpenGNT project made a freely available Greek New Testament possible.

May this project bring Glory to God!
